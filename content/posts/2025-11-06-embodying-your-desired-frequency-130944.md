So, how do we embody our desired frequency? How do we become the vibrational equivalent of what we wish to create? The answer lies in practice, my friend. We must cultivate practices that help us connect with our inner world, that help us listen to the whispers of our soul, and that help us assume the feeling of our desired reality. 

One practice is to simply take a few moments each day to breathe into our desired frequency. We can sit in stillness, close our eyes, and imagine ourselves embodying the vibration we desire. We can feel the sensation of that frequency in our body, allowing it to become a part of our cellular memory. As we practice this, we begin to embody our desired frequency, and our outer reality begins to reflect the harmonic we've become.
