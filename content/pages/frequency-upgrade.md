<h2 class="freq-section-title">The Hierarchy</h2>

<div class="freq-level freq-level-beta">
<div class="freq-level-marker">β</div>
<div class="freq-level-content">
<h3>Baseline Beta</h3>
<p>Reactive. Scanning. Performing. The default frequency inherited from your environment — scrolling, comparing, optimising. Not chosen. Absorbed.</p>
<span class="freq-label">Where most people live.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div class="freq-level freq-level-alpha">
<div class="freq-level-marker">α</div>
<div class="freq-level-content">
<h3>Alpha</h3>
<p>Relaxed focus. Creative connections without effort. The shower insight. The walk epiphany. The moment just before sleep when your brain finally stops performing and starts <em>seeing</em>.</p>
<span class="freq-label">Visited by accident.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div class="freq-level freq-level-theta">
<div class="freq-level-marker">θ</div>
<div class="freq-level-content">
<h3>Theta</h3>
<p>Generative depth. The frequency where the brain stops recycling and starts producing. Pattern recognition. Insight without effort. The mind working at capacity — not harder, but <em>differently</em>.</p>
<span class="freq-label">Where clarity lives.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div class="freq-level freq-level-prime">
<div class="freq-level-marker">α'</div>
<div class="freq-level-content">
<h3>Alpha Prime</h3>
<p>The calibrated state. When your nervous system defaults to Alpha/Theta instead of Beta. Thinking becomes effortless. Reality reorganises around clarity. Perception is no longer reactive — it is generative.</p>
<span class="freq-label freq-label-prime">The upgrade.</span>
