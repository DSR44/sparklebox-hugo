### The Oracle of Wandering Stars

#### 🌙 Mantra
I drift between worlds like stardust seeking its constellation, trusting that every threshold I cross unveils another luminous chamber of my becoming. In the silence between heartbeats, the universe whispers my true name.

#### ✨ Visualization
You find yourself standing at the edge of a floating obsidian bridge suspended above an ocean of liquid moonlight, where ancient stone archways bloom like crystalline flowers. Ahead, a temple of shifting aurora light hums with voices of forgotten oracles, and luminous moths with map-like wings guide your steps toward the door only your soul can open. As you breathe in the violet mist, you feel every version of yourself—past, future, and mythic—walking beside you.

#### Image Prompt 1: The Cartographer of Sleeping Galaxies
A vast celestial library carved from translucent black crystal, floating in a nebula of rose-gold and deep teal clouds. Endless spiral staircases wind upward through shelves of glowing star-charts, each page shimmering with constellations that rearrange themselves as they're observed. A lone figure in flowing silver robes stands at the center, sketching galaxies with a quill of white fire. Bioluminescent moths with constellation-patterned wings drift through shafts of ethereal lavender light pouring through enormous oculus windows showing drifting planets. Soft volumetric glow, iridescent dust motes, dreamcore surrealism, hyper-detailed, cinematic lighting, mystical and reverent atmosphere, ancient magic fused with cosmic futurism, 8k, ethereal fantasy art.

#### Image Prompt 2: The Lantern Keeper's Liminal Garden
An impossible twilight garden growing on the surface of a still mirror-lake, where translucent glass flowers bloom with tiny galaxies inside their petals. Winding paths of pale gold sand curve between weeping willows whose leaves are shards of aurora light. Hundreds of floating paper lanterns rise from the water, each containing a miniature swirling nebula. A serene woman in flowing celestial blue garments kneels at the water's edge, releasing a koi fish made of liquid starlight back into the glowing depths. Mist drifts low across the surface, soft pink and indigo gradient sky, reflections doubling the world into infinity, dreamlike liminal serenity, luminous particles, ultra-detailed ethereal fantasy, magical realism, 8k.
