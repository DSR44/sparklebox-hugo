### The Prism of Forgotten Dawn

#### 🌙 Mantra
I step into the prism of the forgotten dawn, and every light I have ever lost rises to greet me as color. I am the threshold and the sun remembering itself—reborn in hues that have no names yet.

#### ✨ Visualization
You find yourself at the mouth of a corridor built entirely from first light, the air humming like a struck crystal. Each step forward dissolves an old version of you into ribbons of rose gold and violet, weaving them into a dawn that has been waiting for you since before time learned to count. Ahead, the horizon opens like an eye—and you realize you are not walking toward the sunrise; you are the reason it rises.

#### Image Prompt 1: The Refraction Cathedral
A colossal cathedral sculpted from ancient obsidian and living prismatic glass rises from a sea of silver mist at the breathless moment before sunrise. Rivers of uncreated dawn pour through its towering lattice windows, splitting into streams of rose gold, spectral violet, and luminous teal that pool across the mirrored floor. Gigantic moths with stained-glass wings drift between floating stone arches inscribed with glowing sigils that pulse like heartbeats. Bioluminescent vines thread through futuristic copper conduits where tiny stars are born inside delicate glass bulbs. Volumetric light beams cut through drifting fog, illuminating a lone traveler in gossamer robes standing at the threshold, arms open in surrender. Atmosphere: reverent, liminal, tender awe. Dreamcore surrealism, ethereal glow, hyper-detailed, cinematic 8k.

#### Image Prompt 2: The Memory Pool of a Thousand Dawns
At the edge of the prism realm lies a still, mirror-black lake where every dawn ever forgotten floats like luminous lily pads of liquid light. The water reflects not the sky above but a thousand sunrises from worlds that no longer exist—crimson dunes, floating jade islands, golden nebula seas. Koi fish woven from pure aurora light glide beneath the surface, leaving ripples of shimmering ancient script. On the shore, a weathered stone altar cradles an hourglass whose sand falls upward, transforming into small birds of ember and pearl. A white crane with galaxies hidden in its feathers wades through the shallows. Mist curls like slow breath; the air carries rain and old magic. Mood: sacred solitude, gentle melancholy, infinite tenderness. Painterly surrealism, soft glow, ultra-detailed.
